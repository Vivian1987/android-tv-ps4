ANDROID TV 13 FOR PS4 — WORKING RELEASE NOTES
Unfinished notes, not the final release post. Continue collecting Codey's words.

Background
I'm Codey, a PC technician. I previously experimented with Android TV x86,
found BRTDTV, downloaded some of their images, and built Intel PCs into
Android TV media boxes around the house.

I wanted Kayo Sports on PS4 through a NoPSN app, but was tired of the cycle
of waiting for jailbreakable firmware, downloading and running it, obtaining
the retail encryption key, and building the NoPSN app.
I had just missed the scene drop I referred to as 13.6 and 13.52.
Keep those references provisional until I supply the exact wording.

I had previously experimented with CachyOS on my PS4 and modified the BD-J
payload/disc so X launched GoldHEN and Circle booted Linux from another
partition already set up on my internal SSD.
Putting those experiences together made me realise I could likely bring the
Android TV experience to PS4 through a series of tweaks and modifications.
That is what started this project.

Current release
Android TV 13. Newer versions are on the horizon, pending testing.

Development process
I built a setup where AI tools could help make changes, transfer files to
the PS4 over FTP, and send payloads. We could check the results, adjust what
wasn't working, and repeat: a continuous build, test and fix loop on the
console, with me guiding the process.

Accepted wording about AI
I used AI throughout development to help build, test and refine Android TV 13
on PS4. It extended what I could do with my experience and ideas. I'm sharing
it because I'm happy with what I've built and want other people to enjoy it too.

Underlying stance — material for the post, not a lecture
Using AI does not invalidate someone's work. AI can contribute to good or
bad work; its involvement alone does not establish quality either way.
What matters is what is being amplified and whether the person and AI
understand the goals, concepts and boundaries. Neither should pretend to
understand or invent the other's intentions.
